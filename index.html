    How to read these numbers: APR, markout, trading PnL

    Markout APR is what the bins kept from each swap, checked against the pool’s reference price a few minutes later (the horizon: 1, 5 or 30 minutes). It counts the spread and whether the trade was worth taking. Positive means traders paid you; negative means they knew the price was about to move and picked you off.

    Trading PnL compares what you hold now with simply holding the tokens you deposited, at today's prices. Every trade changes your token mix, so when the asset keeps moving after the horizon, trading PnL can be negative while markout is positive.

    Pool APR in the Metric list is the pool’s markout APR over a window. None of these is a forecast.

    Bin Picker turns each bin’s markout back into dollars a year (the window you pick, blended with the last 24 hours, minus a quarter of a negative trading PnL) and divides them by the liquidity in the bin today, plus your deposit. The APR from the API is measured on the bin’s average liquidity over the window; liquidity tends to pile into bins that earned well, so today’s figure is what a new deposit actually shares the flow with. Bins that earn the spread but keep ending up on the wrong side of the move rank lower.

    Read-only: Bin Picker never connects a wallet or asks for a signature. Data from the Metric public API. Markout is a past measurement, not a forecast; providing liquidity can lose money. Not affiliated with Metric. Source
